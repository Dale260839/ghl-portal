## What is newly there to look at

These went in yesterday and are what a local run now shows that it did not
before.

**Dashboard → Team.** Two things are new on this screen:

- The **invite form now asks which projects**, as a tick list, alongside the
  permission ticks. Ticking none is allowed and means they see nothing — the
  safe default for someone you want to add now and assign later.
- **Every existing member has a Projects row** with a "Save projects" button.
  This is the one that matters most right now: without it, assignment could only
  be set at the instant of invitation, so everyone invited before yesterday —
  Dale included — is permanently at zero with no way to correct it.

**The field interface** (`/field`, `/field/tasks`, `/field/update`,
`/field/messages`). All four now resolve visible projects through one helper
rather than four copies. A crew member sees the union of what the contractor
ticked and what they hold a task on.

**The client portal.** An invited homeowner now reads the projects they were
ticked into, by id, instead of a contact lookup that could never match.
