## 1 · "Add to project" failed — the browser was signed in as someone else

`7fb21f8`

On BSA-APS-001's People page, "Add to project" showed the error page with
reference `3533277210`. The Vercel log said `only a contractor can manage the
team`.

**Nothing was wrong with the Hub.** The People page had been opened as the
contractor. Before the click, the same browser signed in as a field crew member
(the crew sign-in test, in another tab or from an invitation link). A browser
holds one sign-in at a time, so the click was sent as the crew member and was
rightly refused.

**What changed:** the error page used to say "the Hub database refused the
write", which sent us looking for a database fault. It now checks who the
browser is signed in as first:

- **Signed in as someone else:** "This browser is signed in as someone else
  now", naming who, how to get back to the contractor view, and to use a
  private window for crew or homeowner tests.
- **Signed out:** says so, with the way back.
- **Right person:** the usual message, unchanged.

**Tip:** test crew or homeowner sign-in in a private window, so your contractor
sign-in stays put.

## 2 · Schedule: tag the homeowner or a crew member, and email the appointment

`ddc1638`

"Trade or crew" on a new appointment is now a dropdown (still optional): the
homeowner, then the field crew on that project.

- **Tagging someone emails the appointment** to the contractor and to that
  person. It sends on a new appointment, or when an edit changes who is tagged;
  fixing a title or date with the same tag sends nothing.
- **Addresses come from the server**, never from the form: the contractor's
  BuildSuite record, the homeowner's contract, the crew member's membership.
- **The homeowner's copy** carries the project code (never the award code) and
  leaves out the team notes and the link, since the appointment is internal
  until released.
- **A crew member with no name set** is shown to the homeowner as "Field crew",
  never by email.
- After saving, the form says who was emailed, or that sending is off.

Checked against a local build with a stand-in Hub and GoHighLevel, so nothing
was really sent.

## 3 · Contractors can assign tasks to the crew

`7edd40e`

**This did not exist.** The database has had task assignment since the start,
and the crew's Tasks screen was ready to show assignments, but nothing could
create a task, so every crew member's Tasks screen was empty.

Each project now has a **Tasks** section in the sidebar, after Schedule:

- **New task:** name, assign to a crew member on that project (or leave
  unassigned), date, trade, and a note the crew sees as "From your PM".
- **Each task** shows who has it and "Not opened yet" or "Seen [date]", and can
  be edited, reassigned, re-statused or removed.
- **Reassigning** shows it as new to the new person; saving without changing
  who keeps their seen state.
- **Only active crew on that project** can be assigned, and only a contractor
  can assign.

Two crew-side bugs fixed on the way: "Got it" was saving to test data, not the
Hub; and the ownership check compared a crew member's *name* to a membership
id, so nobody could ever clear their own "new" badge.

## 4 · The crew can open a task, set its status, and send updates with photos

`375524e`

Reported on prod: a task appeared for the crew member, but it could not be
opened, its status could not be changed, and no image could be added.

- **Each task opens** on its own page: the PM's note, the details, a status
  control, and "Send an update". Opening it clears the "new" badge.
- **An update** goes to Field Updates for PM review, naming the task, e.g.
  "Task: Rough-in electrical, kitchen — Circuits run, boxes set (status set to
  Ready for Review, 1 photo added)".
- **Photos were blocked everywhere.** Every upload was capped at 1 MB, and a
  phone photo is 2–10 MB. Photos are now shrunk on the phone before sending
  (longest side 2000 px) and saved as soon as they are added, with "Take photo"
  and "Choose photos". The Update and Photos screens use the same buttons.
- **A crew member can only act on a task assigned to them**, and photos and
  updates are always filed under that task's project.

**Tested in a browser as a crew member:** a 5.7 MB, 12-megapixel photo arrived
as a 2000×1500 image in under 2 seconds; status and update were saved and
confirmed on screen.

**No database link** exists between a photo or update and a task, so task
photos are captioned "Task: [name]" and updates name the task in their text. A
small migration would let a task list its own photos and updates — say if you
want it.

**To test on prod:**

1. Assign a task to a crew member from the project's Tasks section.
2. On their phone: open it from Tasks, change the status, Save.
3. Send an update with a photo taken on the spot.
4. On your side: status on the Tasks page, update in Field Updates, photo under
   Photos.

## 5 · From the other session

**Invoices use each sub-account's own GoHighLevel token — `db1954e`.** A token
only opens its own sub-account: the AFC token gets 401 on Alliance Pro Services
and the reverse. Invoice calls for APS all failed. New env var
`GHL_LOCATION_TOKENS` (`locationId:token`, comma-separated) supplies tokens for
other sub-accounts. Sign-in, email and data reads still use the main token, so
AFC is unchanged. **Needs the APS entry on Vercel** (top of this page).

**Crew quick actions and the review count — `1ee6bd7`, `db649cd`.**

- The Today and Tasks lists had "Start" and "Complete" buttons that did
  nothing. They now work: "Start" sets In Progress, "Ready for review" sets
  Ready for Review. They reuse the status action from §4.
- Field Updates' "awaiting review" count counted every update; it now counts
  only Pending ones.
- **Worth a look:** the crew's "Suggested client progress summary" on the
  Update screen was being thrown away. It is now saved as a suggestion on the
  update. It stays hidden, and only the PM's publish releases it.
- Submitting an update now checks the project is one of the crew member's.

**GoHighLevel invoice links — `c683edc`.** The "open in GoHighLevel" link for an
invoice pointed at an old URL format. New links use the current editor address,
and saved drafts' links are upgraded when shown.

## 8 · The thing worth keeping

Three of today's problems were invisible until someone used the product as a
crew member would. Every phone photo hit a 1 MB limit nobody had set on
purpose. Task ownership compared a name to an id, so no crew member could ever
clear a badge. And an error page blamed the database for what was really two
sign-ins in one browser. None of them failed a test, because each test checked
its own piece. Walking the whole path, as the person holding the phone, is what
found them.
