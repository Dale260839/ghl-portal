## 1 · The PM is told when the crew submit — `bd5bc70`

Nothing announced a submission before. An update landed in the review queue and
the only way to learn about it was to go and look.

Every crew submission — a daily update or an update sent from a task — now
emails the contractor's address on file: the project, who sent it, what they
wrote, how many photos, and a link to the queue. **A blocker leads the subject
line.** The crew's own screen says whether the email actually went, because
"saved" and "they know" are different facts and a person on a roof deserves the
true one.

A blocker also opens a real **Issue** now, which is what the field form has
always promised. The workflow planned that from the start and the fixture port
only ever wrote it to the log.

## 2 · A task owns its photos and updates — `bd5bc70`, `e47ea8f`

A photo taken on a task and an update sent from it were tied to the task only
by the words "Task: …" in the caption — readable by a person, matchable by
nothing, and §3.6 is explicit that a cross-record link is never keyed on a
title. Migration 0015 adds the two columns, and a task now lists its own photos
and updates on the crew's task screen and under each task on your Tasks page.

## 3 · The portal stops pretending — `e47ea8f`

Nine buttons in the homeowner's portal did nothing at all. Every one is now
either real or gone:

- **Raise an issue** is a form. It arrives on your Issues page marked as raised
  by the client, and stays visible to them with its status — a request that
  vanishes on submission looks lost.
- **Upload File** is an uploader: photos (shrunk on their phone, like the
  crew's) and PDFs, landing in the project's **Client** folder labelled
  "From <name>".
- Both are gated three ways: the portal master switch, the project's own switch
  — **Allow Issue Submission** and **Allow File Uploads**, both new on the
  Visibility screen and both off by default — and the project coming from their
  own memberships, never from the form.
- **Request a change**, **Ask about the schedule**, **Review selections**,
  **Review change orders**, **See payments** and **Reply about this update**
  now go where the job is actually done. **Sync Calendar** and a **Confirm
  Access** button that was invisible anyway are gone.

A guardrail now fails if any placeholder button returns to the portal.

## 4 · From the other session — `28a1543`, `daad0df`

- **Duplicate invoices cannot be created.** Each attempt claims the draft in the
  database first, so an interrupted request locks rather than risking a second

  This is what migration 0014 was for.
- **The invoice business address** is mapped into GoHighLevel's address object
  rather than sent as a single string.
